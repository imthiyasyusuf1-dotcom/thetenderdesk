The story

An engineer who builds for local business.

I'm a Chemical Engineering student at London South Bank University, graduating in 2027. Engineering taught me to break a problem down, measure it and fix what actually matters. I apply the same approach to small businesses.

I started The Tender Desk in Aldershot after seeing good local firms lose out. They had great reviews but slow, dated websites. They could do council work but never bid for it. They spent evenings answering the same enquiries by hand.

So I built a business around three fixes. Websites that turn searches into bookings. Tenders that open up public sector contracts. AI that handles the admin. Every site I make is hand built, fast on a phone and yours to own.
