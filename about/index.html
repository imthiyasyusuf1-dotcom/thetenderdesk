Why it exists

I kept seeing good businesses lose work they should have won. Great reviews, slow, dated websites. Capable of public sector contracts, never bidding for them. So I started a desk that fixes both.

01 / The story

An engineer who builds for business.

Trained to measure.

I'm a final year BEng Chemical Engineering student at London South Bank University. Engineering taught me to break a problem down, measure it and fix what actually matters.

Before The Tender Desk I worked in a lab at Element Materials Technology, running SAR testing. Precise work, where a missed detail means a wrong result. I bring that same care to every page and every bid.

Built to win work.

A website is often the first handshake. A tender is often the biggest door. When either is weak, the work goes to someone else, and the business rarely finds out why.

So I built one studio around the things that bring work in: sites people remember, bids that score, ads that land and AI that answers when you can't.
