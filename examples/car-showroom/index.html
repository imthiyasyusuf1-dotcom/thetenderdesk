How every car is prepared

Nothing reaches the floor until it is right.

Our preparation takes up to three weeks per car. If a car fails any step, we do not sell it.

  1. History and provenanceFinance, write off and mileage checks, every invoice and stamp read through.
  2. 160 point inspectionRamp, road test and diagnostic scan by our own technicians.
  3. Service and consumablesFluids, brakes and tyres brought up to standard, with the paperwork.
  4. Paint and interiorMachine polish, ceramic protection, leather cleaned and fed.
