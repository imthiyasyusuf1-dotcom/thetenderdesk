Questions

How long does a kitchen take to fit?

Most kitchens are fitted in two to three weeks once cabinets arrive, with stone worktops measured and installed in the final week.

Can you work with my existing layout?

Yes. Many of our rooms keep the plumbing where it is, which saves time. We will tell you honestly when moving things is worth it.

Do you handle the plumbing, electrics and tiling?

All of it, by our own fitters and certified electricians. You deal with your designer and nobody else.

Do I need an appointment for the showroom?

Walk ins are welcome, but booking means a designer is free to sit down with you for the full hour.
