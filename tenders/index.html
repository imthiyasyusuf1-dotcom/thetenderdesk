Publiccontracts,written to win.

Clear, compliant bids for small businesses. You do the work. We find the right contracts, read the spec properly and write answers that follow the scoring criteria. Every bid is quoted individually, with a free go or no go first.

Councils, NHS trusts, housing associations and schools buy cleaning, repairs, care and transport every week. Plenty of small firms could deliver that work and never bid, because the paperwork looks like a second job. That paperwork is our job.

01 Who it’s for

Small firms who do good work.

SMEs bidding for council, NHS, housing association and education contracts, whether it is your first tender or your tenth.

02 What we do

Everything between the notice and submit.

01

Finding opportunities

We watch the public portals for contracts that fit your size and sector, so you only see the ones worth a look.

02

Go or no go

An honest read of the spec, the scoring and the pass or fail criteria before you spend a minute writing. If it is not winnable, we say so.

03

Reading the spec

Every requirement, deadline and clarification pulled into one checklist so nothing gets missed.

04

Method statements

How you will actually deliver the contract, written clearly from what you tell us about how you work.

05

Quality answers

Each question answered against its marking scheme, inside the word limit, with your real evidence.

06

Social value

Jobs, apprenticeships, suppliers and carbon commitments you can genuinely keep, written to the buyer’s model.

07

PQQ, SQ and frameworks

Selection questionnaires and framework applications completed and checked, so you reach the scored stage.

08

Compliance checks

Every mandatory document, declaration, policy and format rule checked before you submit.

09

Bid library

Your best answers, policies and case studies saved so every next bid is faster.

03 How it works

Four steps. One deadline.

01

Send the tender

Forward the notice or documents. No tender yet? We will find contracts that fit.

02

Go or no go

A free, straight read of what it scores and whether it is worth bidding.

03

Write

A short call to gather your experience, people and processes, then drafts of every answer.

04

Check and submit

A full compliance check, final edits with you, and submission well before the deadline.
