02 / What I do

Everything between the notice and the submit button.

Finding opportunities

I watch the public portals for contracts that fit your size, sector and patch, so you only see the ones worth a look.

Go / no-go

An honest read of the spec, the scoring and the pass/fail criteria before you spend a minute writing. If it's not winnable, I'll say so.

Reading the spec

Every requirement, deadline and clarification pulled into one checklist so nothing gets missed.

Method statements

How you'll actually deliver the contract, written clearly from what you tell me about how you work.

Quality answers

Each question answered against its marking scheme, inside the word limit, with your real evidence.

Social value

Local jobs, apprenticeships, suppliers and carbon commitments you can genuinely keep, written to the buyer's model.

PQQ, SQ and frameworks

Selection questionnaires and framework applications completed and checked, so you get through to the scored stage.

Compliance checks

Every mandatory document, declaration, policy and format rule checked against the instructions before you submit.

03 / How it works

Four steps. One deadline.

  1. 01

    Send the tender

    Forward the notice or the documents. If you don't have one yet, I'll find contracts that fit.

  2. 02

    Go / no-go

    A free, straight read of the spec: what it scores, what could rule you out and whether it's worth bidding.

  3. 03

    Write

    A short call to get your experience, people and processes, then drafts of every answer for you to check.

  4. 04

    Check and submit

    A full compliance check against the instructions, final edits with you, and submission well before the deadline.
