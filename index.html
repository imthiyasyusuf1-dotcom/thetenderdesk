Why you are not winning public work

It is not the quality of your work. It is that the contracts are published on portals you do not check, the paperwork is written for firms with a bid team, and the consultants who could help price themselves for companies ten times your size.

You never see them

Since February 2025 public bodies publish on the government's Central Digital Platform. Unless someone is checking it daily for your trade and your area, the contract is awarded before you know it existed.

The forms put you off

Selection questionnaires, method statements, social value, policies, insurance evidence. None of it is hard once it is written down properly the first time. It just never gets written down.

Help is priced out of reach

Bid writers charge 450 to 750 pounds a day and 2,000 to 8,000 pounds a tender. That works for a firm with 100 staff. It does not work for you.

What we will and will not promise

We do not guarantee that any bid is won. Nobody honest can. What we promise is that you will see every contract you are eligible for, that every bid we submit is complete, on time and based only on things your firm can evidence, and that you can stop at any time. Our drafting is AI assisted and checked by a person before anything is sent, and we will tell you plainly when we think a contract is not worth your time.
