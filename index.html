New revenue: public contracts

Councils buy from local firms. Few ever bid.

Public contracts can add a steady income stream alongside your usual customers. Most small firms lose them before anyone reads a word. Here is where it goes wrong.

  1. You never see them

    Since February 2025 buyers publish on the government's Central Digital Platform. If nobody checks it daily for your trade and your patch, the contract is awarded before you knew it existed.

  2. The forms stop you

    Selection questionnaires, method statements, social value, policies, insurance evidence. None of it is hard once it is written down properly. It just never gets written down.

  3. Help is priced for someone else

    Bid consultancies charge £450 to £750 a day and £2,000 to £8,000 a tender. That works for a firm with a hundred staff. It does not work for you.

Tenders: how it works

One call to set up. A short email every week.

You stay on the tools. We do the watching, the reading and the writing.

  1. We register you properly

    Central Digital Platform account, supplier profile, share code. Done once, done right, so you are eligible to bid from day one.

  2. We watch every portal daily

    Find a Tender, Contracts Finder and your regional portal, filtered to your trade, your area and the contract sizes you can actually deliver.

  3. You get one page a week

    Only the contracts worth your time, each with a plain summary, the deadline, and our honest view of your chances. Nothing else.

  4. We write the bid with you

    Every answer drafted from your own evidence, checked line by line, formatted and submitted before the deadline. You approve it first.

    Questions

    The things people ask on the first call.

    How do you actually make me more money?

    By fixing the places you lose customers. That might be a site nobody books from, a Google listing nobody sees, enquiries that go unanswered after hours, no-shows, or public contracts you never hear about. We find which of those apply to you and fix those first.

    Can you promise how much extra I will make?

    No, and be wary of anyone who does. Every business is different. What we can do is measure calls, enquiries, bookings and reviews before and after, so you can see for yourself whether it is paying off.

    Do I have to buy everything?

    No. Each service stands on its own. Plenty of businesses only need a better website, a proper Google listing or faster replies to enquiries. We will tell you which one is likely to matter most.

    Are public contracts really open to a firm my size?

    Yes. Buyers mark many notices as suitable for small and medium firms, and the government has a published plan to send more work their way. Small and medium firms took £45.2 billion of public spend in 2025. The contracts we send you are filtered by value so you only see work you could deliver with the staff you have.

    What if I have never bid for public work before?

    That is the normal starting point. The first job is getting you registered and tender ready, which is a one off. After that you are eligible for everything your trade covers.

    Do you use AI?

    Yes, for enquiry assistants, reading tender notices and first drafts. Every line is checked by a person before anything reaches your customers or a buyer. That keeps the work quick without cutting corners.

    Are the sites on your work page real clients?

    Some are. 7ei8ht6 Properties is a real client site, live on their own domain. The others are concept and demo builds so you can see the quality before deciding anything. Crumble & Co is a concept we made for a real Aldershot dessert shop; the rest are fictional businesses.

    Will an AI assistant say the wrong thing to my customers?

    It is set up to answer from information you give it and to pass anything it is unsure of to you. You see and approve what it says before it goes live.

    How do I pay, and can I stop?

    Every project is quoted individually after a short call, and you get the figure in writing before anything starts. Monthly services are paid by card or direct debit and are rolling, so you can stop whenever you like with no notice period and no exit fee.
