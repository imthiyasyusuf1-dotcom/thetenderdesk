Chapter I

The Major

Major Noor served with courage and distinction in the WWII Burma Campaign, one of the most challenging military efforts in history, where Indian soldiers fought alongside British and Allied forces through tropical heat, scarcity and sacrifice.

A taste of home
Chapter II

The craving

Wherever he travelled with his regiment, he longed for the fiery heat of Indian chillies, the aromatic blend of garlic and the refreshing coolness of mint. He could never find anything quite like it.

Those flavours meant more than food. They were a taste of identity, resilience and belonging.

ChilliGarlicMint
Imran and Abbas
Chapter III

The grandsons

More than 85 years later, his grandsons Imran and Abbas rekindled that legacy, recreating his favourites from family memories. Firecracker Naga chillies for natural heat, fresh mint leaves for a cooling finish, handcrafted with care.

“From the battlefields of Burma to tables around the world.”

Good questions

How hot is it?

Medium to hot. Firecracker Naga and bird's eye chilli heat, cooled by fresh mint.

What do I eat it with?

Wraps and shawarma, rice bowls, potatoes, pizza, grilled chicken, burgers, or straight up as a dip.

Who was Major Noor?

An officer of the British Indian Army who served in the WWII Burma Campaign. His grandsons Imran and Abbas recreated the sauces he craved.
