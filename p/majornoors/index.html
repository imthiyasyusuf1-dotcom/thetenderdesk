BURMA1944

Chapter one

Ours begins over eight decades ago, in the jungles of Burma.

Major Noor served with courage and distinction in the British Indian Army during the Burma Campaign, one of the most demanding theatres of the Second World War.

Far from home, he longed for the fiery heat of Indian chillies and the refreshing coolness of mint. Wherever he travelled with his regiment, he could never find anything quite like his homeland's chilli and garlic sauces.

Chapter two

His grandsons
brought it home.

Imran and Abbas recreated their grandfather's favourites and served them in their Rooster Shack restaurants. Customers loved them so much, there was only one thing left to say.

Questions,

How hot is it?

Firecracker hot up front from Bird's Eye chilli and green jalapeños, then fresh mint cools the finish.

What do I eat it with?

Chicken, wraps, rice, samosas, kebabs. One sauce, endless combinations.

Where does the recipe come from?

It was inspired by Major Noor, who served in the British Indian Army during the Second World War. His grandsons recreated his favourite flavours for their Rooster Shack restaurants before bottling them.

Who does my bottle support?

10p from every bottle sold goes to the Royal British Veterans Enterprise, supporting veteran-led businesses.

Can I buy outside the UK?

We are expanding into the UAE and GCC markets soon.
