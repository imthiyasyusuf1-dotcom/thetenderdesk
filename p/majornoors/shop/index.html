Ingredients

Water, Tomato Purée, Crushed green jalapeños (6%), Bird's Eye Chilli Flakes (4%), Sugar, Fresh Mint (2.7%), Acetic Acid (Vinegar), Ground spices (1%), Natural Thickener, Potassium Sorbate (to maintain freshness).

Allergy advice: for allergens including cereals containing gluten, see ingredients in bold.

The story

As a Major in the British Indian Army, Noor missed his homeland's delicious chilli and garlic sauces. Wherever he travelled with his regiment, he could never find anything quite like it. Now, many years later, his grandson Imran has recreated the authentic flavours loved by his grandfather.

What goes in

Heat first. Then the mint.

Bird's Eye chilli flakes and crushed green jalapeños bring intense, natural heat. Fresh mint lands a cooling finish, so the heat never outstays its welcome.
