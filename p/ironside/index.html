Questions

How is my order delivered?

Orders are packed in our warehouse and sent by courier. Delivery cost and times are confirmed with your order.

Are prices inc VAT?

Prices are shown ex VAT by default. Use the Ex VAT / Inc VAT switch at the top of any page to see prices with 20% VAT added.

How do trade accounts work?

Prices are set for everyone. If you buy regularly, apply for a trade account and we will come back to you about better pricing.

Can I get test certificates?

Yes. Ask us for the test certificate for any product and we will send what we hold.

What about returns?

See our delivery and returns page. Contact us before sending anything back.
