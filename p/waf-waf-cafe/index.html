Our story

It all started in our kitchen.

What began as a small lockdown project quickly turned into a passion that brought joy to our friends, neighbours and community.

  • 2020

    During lockdown we started making and selling homemade food from our own kitchen.

  • 2021

    We took the next step and opened our very own café. Since then we have run it together as a couple, pouring our hearts into every dish, every coffee and every smile we serve.

“For us, this café is more than just a business. It’s a place built on love, creativity and the happiness that comes from sharing good moments.”

Cansu & Uğur
