Straight answers.

How much does contract cleaning cost?

Every site is different, so we price after a free site survey. You get a written plan and a fixed monthly quote with no obligation.

Can you clean outside our working hours?

Yes. Our teams are local and we are open 24 hours, so cleans can happen early morning, evenings, overnight or at weekends.

Do you work with schools, NHS and care settings?

Yes. We work across education, healthcare, care homes and housing associations, following strict hygiene routines and working around term times and residents.

What if our needs change during the contract?

We stay flexible. Frequencies and tasks can be adjusted for seasons, events or changes in how you use the building.

Which areas do you cover?

Hampshire, Surrey and Berkshire for regular contracts, including Farnborough, Aldershot, Fleet, Camberley, Farnham, Guildford, Woking and Basingstoke, with specialist cleaning programmes delivered nationally.

What accreditations do your teams hold?

Our operatives hold IPAF and CSCS cards and City and Guilds training. We are a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, and our drainage team works to Water Jetting Association standards.
