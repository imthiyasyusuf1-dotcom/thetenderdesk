Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does a conversion take?

Most conversions are completed within one to two days. We can often start first thing in the morning and have your car ready by close of business the following day.

Can I switch between petrol and LPG?

Yes. It's a dual fuel system, so you can switch between petrol and LPG whenever you need to.

Will I lose boot space?

Not necessarily. A toroidal tank fits in the spare wheel well, or a cylindrical tank can be fitted for more capacity. We'll talk you through the options for your car.

Do you handle UK registration?

Yes. We handle certification and DVLA documentation for you.

What do you need from me for a quote?

Your registration, make, model, engine size and mileage. If you know whether the engine is turbocharged or direct injection, that helps too.

Do you offer a courtesy car?

Not at the moment, but most conversions are done within one to two days.
