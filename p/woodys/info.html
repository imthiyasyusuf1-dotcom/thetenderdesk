    FAQs

    How long will my order take?

    Collection is usually ready in 15 to 20 minutes. Delivery is usually 35 to 50 minutes, longer on Friday and Saturday nights. You can also pick a time later today at checkout.

    Where do you deliver?

    Is there a service charge?

    No. You pay the menu price, plus delivery if you choose it. Tips are optional and go to the team.

    How do I pay?

    Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay online, or cash when you collect or on delivery.

    Can I order ahead?

    Yes. Choose a time at checkout, even when the shop is closed, and it will be cooked for that time.

    Something wrong with my order?

    Call the branch straight away with your order reference and they will put it right.

    Do I need an account?

    No, you can check out as a guest. An account saves your details and lets you repeat past orders in one tap.

    Allergens

    Our food is cooked fresh in small kitchens that handle all 14 major allergens, including gluten, milk, egg, mustard, sesame, soya, celery and crustaceans (the prawn sauce). We cannot promise any dish is free from traces.

    If you have an allergy or intolerance, please call your branch before you order. They will talk you through every ingredient. You can also add a note to any item.

    Halal status, oils and the full allergen matrix to be confirmed by Woody's before launch.
