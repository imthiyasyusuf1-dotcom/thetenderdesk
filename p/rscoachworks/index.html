About us

A garage the way it should be.

We're an independent garage built on a simple idea: be honest, work hard and be reliable. We rely on repeat customers and word of mouth, so we repair your car properly the first time and for a fair price.

Whether you need one tyre replaced or a major bodywork repair, you get the same attention and care. We work on all makes and models, and we're a 5 to 10 minute drive from the city centre, so dropping off and collecting is easy.
