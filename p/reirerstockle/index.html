The maker

Reiner Stöckle

Reiner served his apprenticeship in Immenstadt, Germany, a renowned centre for traditional crafts, and is now a certified Master Craftsman.

Traditional joinery techniques, modern technology, and meticulous attention to detail.

Before founding RS Cabinet Maker he worked as senior cabinet maker and project manager on Huf Houses, where only the most exacting workmanship is accepted. Today the workshop designs and makes bespoke joinery for homes and businesses across the North East, with a personable approach and fixed project deadlines.
