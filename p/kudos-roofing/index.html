Roofing questions.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Every quote is free and no obligation. Call 07973 193248 or send the form and we will come and look at the roof.

What guarantee do I get?

All our work comes with a minimum 10 year guarantee. Higher end specification flat roofs come with a 20 year guarantee.

What flat roof systems do you fit?

Single ply, high performance felt, GRP and Firestone rubber roofing, for both repairs and full renewals.

Do you need to hire in scaffolding?

No. We run our own in-house scaffolding service, so access is arranged around your job.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Farnborough and cover Aldershot, Fleet, Camberley, Frimley, Farnham, Yateley, Hook, Woking, Guildford, Basingstoke, Reading and beyond across Hampshire, Surrey and Berkshire.

Do you work on commercial roofs?

Yes. We are a small family business with over 25 years of trading, handling both domestic and commercial roofing.
