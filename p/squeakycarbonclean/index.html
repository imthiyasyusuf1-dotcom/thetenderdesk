What carbon cleaning does

A deep clean for the bits you never see.

Your engine burns fuel and air. Over time some of that leaves a hard black crust behind. Carbon cleaning breaks that crust down and carries it out through the exhaust, so air and fuel can flow the way the engine was designed for.

01

Engine carbon clean

Shifts carbon from the intake, valves and combustion chamber on petrol and diesel engines.

02

DPF cleaning

For diesels whose particulate filter is clogging up, throwing warning lights or will not regenerate.

03

EGR and intake

Helps clear the soot that gums up the EGR valve and inlet, a common cause of rough running.

04

Diagnostics first

We plug in and read the fault codes before and after, so you know what was going on.

Signs your car needs it

Sound familiar?

These are the things people usually message us about. If one of them sounds like your car, send us your reg.

DPF

DPF light on or limp mode

The filter is clogged with soot and the car is protecting itself by cutting power.

EGR

EGR faults and rough idle

A sooty EGR valve can stick, causing shudders, stalling and engine management lights.

Power

Lost power and flat spots

Hesitates when you pull away or struggles up hills it used to take easily.

Smoke

Black or grey smoke

Puffs of smoke when you accelerate are a classic sign of carbon build up.

MPG

Fuel going further than it should

Filling up more often than you used to, with no change in how you drive.

MOT

MOT emissions worries

Failed on emissions, or worried about the smoke test coming up. A clean before the test is worth a chat.
