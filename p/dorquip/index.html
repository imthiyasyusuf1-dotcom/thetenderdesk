00What sets Dorquip apart

Over more than 40 years we've built our reputation on a collaborative spirit, and on helping customers define exactly what they need so we can meet it precisely and quickly.

Dorquip provides architectural ironmongery, washroom hardware and hygiene products for interior fitout, joinery, main contractor and facilities management businesses.

Whether it's an end to end specification or a custom build for one specific need, we respond quickly and accurately to urgent requests and complex briefs, and we manage the supply chain so precisely the right hardware turns up on time.

04Case study

Oxford Biochemistry,
Phase 2.

Building
£70m
Started
March 2019
Client
University of Oxford
Scope
Door hardware

The Department of Biochemistry needed more space for specialised equipment. Phase 2 had to carry the finish of Phase 1 through, guarantee a long serviceable life with complete fire safe integrity, and give storage rooms controlled access that's easy to use under strict security policies.

The Dorquip solution

  1. Worked directly with the architect and main contractor on every detail.
  2. Produced a qualified ironmongery schedule aligned with the drawings.
  3. Supplied door preparation samples so the joinery contractor morticed every door and frame for the right hardware.
  4. Packed ironmongery into door-sets and built delivery schedules with the main contractor, around other trades and campus life.
