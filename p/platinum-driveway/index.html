Questions

Good to know.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Every quote is free and no obligation. Call 07830 226562 or send a photo on WhatsApp and Luke will arrange a look at the surface.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Farnborough and work across Hampshire, Surrey and Berkshire, including Aldershot, Fleet, Farnham, Camberley, Frimley, Guildford, Woking, Bracknell, Wokingham and Reading.

What surfaces can you clean?

Block paving, imprinted concrete, tarmac, sandstone and other patio slabs, timber decking and commercial grounds such as car parks and forecourts.

How long does a driveway clean take?

Anywhere from a few hours to a few days, depending on the size of the area and whether it is being re-sanded and sealed.

Why seal block paving after cleaning?

After cleaning, kiln dried sand goes back into the joints and a sealer is applied. This helps stop staining and slows moss, algae and weed growth so the drive stays cleaner for longer.

How much does driveway cleaning cost?

Every job is different, so we give a free quote after seeing the surface. There is no obligation.
