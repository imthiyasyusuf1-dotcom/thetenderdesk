Aster Bay Residences

Life, framed by the seafront

Three slender towers with sculpted, wave-shaped balconies rise from a landscaped podium, a private pool and the beach beyond. One hundred and thirty two residences, each with its own horizon.

01

Balconies shaped by the tide

Every floor shifts a little further than the last, so the towers ripple against the sky and every terrace gets its own sweep of sea.

02

A podium made for slow days

A landscaped garden deck, a long reflecting pool and shaded loungers sit one level above the promenade, with the sand a few steps beyond.

03

Golden hour, every evening

Seaward glazing, deep soffits and generous ceiling heights turn the last hour of daylight into the best part of living here.
