FAQ

Common questions

Do you really charge no call-out fee?

Yes, Monday to Friday 8am–6pm there is no call-out charge. You only pay the fixed price for the job, agreed before we start. Out-of-hours rates are listed in the pricing guide.

How fast can you get to an emergency?

Our target is 60 minutes across our core area, and most emergencies are reached sooner. We'll give you a live ETA by text once an engineer is assigned.

What should I do while I wait for a leak?

Turn off your stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink), switch off the boiler and any electrics near the water, and open cold taps to drain the system. Our assistant can walk you through it.

Are your engineers qualified for gas work?

All gas work is carried out by registered gas engineers, and you can check their ID card on arrival. (On this demo the registration details are placeholders.)

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or Apple/Google Pay on completion. Finance options are available on new boilers.

Is the work guaranteed?

Every job carries a 12-month guarantee on parts and labour. New boilers carry the manufacturer's warranty on top.
