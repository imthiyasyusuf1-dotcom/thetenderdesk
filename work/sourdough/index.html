Our story

Four ingredients.
No shortcuts.

“Flour, water, salt, time. The first three are cheap. The fourth is the whole point.”

Rise & Crust started as a starter in a jam jar and a Saturday stall. Six years on we still mix every dough by hand the day before, let it slowly ferment overnight, and bake from 4am so the loaves are still crackling when the door opens.

We buy stoneground flour from a mill forty miles away, use proper butter, and stop baking when we sell out. That's it.

  1. Day oneLevain fed, dough mixed and folded by hand.
  2. OvernightA long, cold ferment for flavour and digestibility.
  3. 4amShaped, scored, baked hot in a stone-deck oven.

— Mara & Tom, bakers
