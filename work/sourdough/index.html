How a loaf gets made

Slow is the whole recipe.

Long fermentation does the work that additives do in supermarket bread. It's why ours tastes of something, and why it's kinder on your gut.

0hrs

Feed

Doris gets stone-milled flour and water, same as every day since 2019.

4hrs

Mix & fold

By hand, four sets of folds, until the dough is glossy and full of air.

20hrs

Cold proof

Overnight at 4°C. This is where the flavour and the open crumb come from.

36hrs

Bake

Into the deck oven at 4:30am, hot and steamy, for a crust that sings as it cools.

Good to know

Can I order for the same day?

Online orders close at 8pm the night before so we know how much to bake. On the day, just pop in: there's always plenty on the counter until about 1pm.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Our brownie is gluten-free by recipe, but everything is made in a kitchen that handles wheat flour all day, so we can't promise it's safe for coeliacs.

Is there seating?

A few window stools and a bench outside. Dogs welcome; there's a water bowl by the door.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants?

Yes, a handful of local spots. Ask at the counter and we'll talk wholesale.
