What we do

Everything outside the back door.

  1. 01Garden designA measured survey, a scaled plan and a planting scheme you can walk through in 3D before anything is dug.
  2. 02Hard landscapingPorcelain and natural stone terraces, paths, steps and walls, laid on proper foundations with drainage built in.
  3. 03PlantingBorders, hedging and specimen trees sourced from specialist nurseries and planted by our own team.
  4. 04Water and lightingReflecting pools, rills and low voltage lighting designed as part of the scheme, not added at the end.
  5. 05Outdoor livingKitchens, fire pits, pergolas and garden rooms for evenings outside.
  6. 06AftercareSeasonal maintenance visits so the garden grows into the design instead of away from it.

How it works

Four steps from sketch to soil.

Step 1Garden visit

We walk the space with you, talk through how you want to use it and take measurements.

Step 2Design

A scaled plan, planting palette and 3D views, refined together until it feels right.

Step 3Build

One crew from groundworks to final planting, with a clear programme and a tidy site.

Step 4Grow in

Aftercare visits through the first year so everything settles in and thrives.
