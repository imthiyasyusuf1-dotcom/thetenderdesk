FAQ

Straight answers.

Still stuck? .

Is there a joining fee or contract?

No joining fee. Monthly plans are a 30-day rolling contract, so you can cancel with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are paid up front with two months free.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Around a third of our members had never used a barbell before joining. Every membership starts with a 1:1 induction and a plan built around you.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for up to 3 months a year for injury, travel or anything else. It's free and done in the app.

How do I book classes?

Through the IronCore app. Booking opens 7 days ahead and you can cancel up to 2 hours before without penalty.

Is there parking and changing rooms?

Free parking for 40 cars, plus showers, lockers and changing rooms with hair dryers.

Do you offer discounts?

15% off for students, NHS staff and the Armed Forces. Bring ID to your induction.
