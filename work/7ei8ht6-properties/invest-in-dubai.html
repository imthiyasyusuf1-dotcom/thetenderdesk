Why Dubai

The fundamentals,
with sources.الأساسيات
مع المصادر

We only state what can be checked. Each point links to its public source. Rules change, so we confirm the current position with you before any decision.

  1. 0%

    No personal income tax

    The UAE does not levy income tax on individuals. VAT at 5% applies to goods and services, and corporate tax applies to businesses, so structure matters if you buy through a company.

    Source: UAE Government portal, Taxation
  2. AED 2M

    Golden Visa through property

    Real estate investors with a minimum capital of AED 2 million are eligible for a 5 year, renewable Golden Visa, which needs no sponsor and allows family members to be sponsored.

    Source: UAE Government portal, Golden visa
  3. Freehold

    Outright ownership in designated areas

    In Dubai, foreign ownership is permitted in areas designated as freehold, whether or not the buyer lives in the UAE. Title deeds are issued by the Dubai Land Department.

    Source: UAE Government portal, Buying property in Dubai
  4. 4%

    Dubai Land Department transfer fee

    A transfer fee of 4% of the price is payable to the Dubai Land Department on registration, the rate set in 2013. Budget for it alongside agency and trustee fees.

    Source: Economy of Dubai, citing the DLD fee change
  5. RERA

    Regulation and escrow

    The Real Estate Regulatory Agency, part of the Dubai Land Department, licenses brokers, regulates developers and manages developers' trust accounts: the escrow that holds off-plan payments.

    Source: Real Estate Regulatory Agency

Dubai rewards local knowledge and punishes haste. Our role is to supply the first and prevent the second.

Residency

The Golden Visa,
considered early.الإقامة الذهبية

If residence is part of the plan, it shapes what you buy. The UAE Golden Visa for real estate investors requires a minimum investment of AED 2 million and is issued for 5 years, renewable. We flag which options meet the threshold at the shortlist stage and introduce you to the right specialists for the application.

Eligibility is decided by the UAE authorities. Nothing on this page is legal or tax advice.
