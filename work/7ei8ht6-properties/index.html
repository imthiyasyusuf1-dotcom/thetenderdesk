Why Dubai

Four facts that matter
to an international buyer.أربع حقائق تهم
المستثمر الدولي

0%
Personal income tax. The UAE does not levy income tax on individuals.
AED 2M
The property investment from which an owner may be eligible for the UAE Golden Visa.
Freehold
Foreign buyers may own outright in Dubai's designated freehold areas, with title issued by the Land Department.
RERA
Brokers, developers and off-plan escrow accounts are regulated by the Real Estate Regulatory Agency.
