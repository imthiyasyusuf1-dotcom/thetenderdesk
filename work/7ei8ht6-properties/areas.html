Area guide

Dubai, district by district.مناطق دبي

Six districts we work in every week. Each has a different buyer, a different tenant and a different reason to hold.

Downtown DubaiDubai MarinaPalm JumeirahBusiness BayDubai HillsMirdif & Nadd Al Hamar
01 / 06

وسط مدينة دبي

Downtown Dubai

Home to the Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building, with The Dubai Mall and the Dubai Fountain at its foot. Largely apartments, from compact units to penthouses, in a walkable centre developed by Emaar.

Suited toBuyers who want Dubai's most recognisable address, and the visitor demand that comes with it.

Ask about Downtown Dubai
02 / 06

دبي مارينا

Dubai Marina

A waterfront district built around a man-made canal, lined with residential towers and the Marina Walk promenade, a short walk from the beach at JBR and served by tram and metro.

Suited toApartment investors, and residents who want the water and the beach close by.

Ask about Dubai Marina
03 / 06

نخلة جميرا

Palm Jumeirah

The palm-shaped island off Jumeirah: villas on the fronds with their own beach frontage, apartments along the trunk, and resort hotels on the crescent, Atlantis among them.

Suited toFamily villas, trophy homes and beachfront apartments.

Ask about Palm Jumeirah
04 / 06

الخليج التجاري

Business Bay

The business district beside Downtown, set along the Dubai Water Canal. Offices, hotels and apartment towers share the waterfront, with Downtown across the bridge.

Suited toInvestors after central apartments, and companies looking for offices near the centre.

Ask about Business Bay
05 / 06

دبي هيلز

Dubai Hills

A master-planned community by Emaar and Meraas between Downtown and the Marina, laid out around an 18-hole championship golf course, with Dubai Hills Park and Dubai Hills Mall.

Suited toFamilies looking for villas and townhouses with green space, and buyers of new apartments.

Ask about Dubai Hills
06 / 06

مردف وند الحمر

Mirdif & Nadd Al Hamar

Established, low-rise neighbourhoods near Dubai International Airport: villas and townhouses, Mirdif City Centre, and the wide lawns of Mushrif Park. Our office is here, at Amaya Plaza.

Suited toFamilies who want space and quiet streets, and landlords who want steady long-term tenants.

Ask about Mirdif & Nadd Al Hamar
