    Your local dessert parlour

    Looking for desserts nearby? Crumble & Co is in the town centre, serving waffles, crepes, kunafa, Dutch pancakes, brioche French toast, milk cakes and soft serve until late. Easy to reach, with delivery across the area.

    Good to know

    Quick answers

    Can I order ahead and collect?

    Yes. Order online and pick up from the shop, a short walk from the station.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Order through the Crumble & Co ordering page for delivery or collection.

    Can I just walk in and sit down?

    Yes, no booking needed. Open from 11am every day, until 10pm Monday to Wednesday and 11pm Thursday to Sunday.

    Do you do celebration orders?

    Call 07345 532899 or message @crumbleand_co on Instagram with your date, numbers and flavour ideas.

    What about allergens?

    Many desserts contain nuts, dairy, gluten and egg. Please speak to staff about allergens before ordering.
